# Backlink Playbook — MyAstro360

**Why this document exists:** the site's technical SEO is in good shape, but the
domain has effectively **zero backlink authority** (DR 0; the only "referring
site" GSC shows is jyotron.com — historical residue from when that dead domain
served this same app, worth nothing). At DR 0, Google crawls shallowly (hence
hundreds of "Discovered – currently not indexed" pages) and won't rank the site
for competitive head terms no matter how good the on-page work is. **Link
building is the single highest-leverage SEO activity available**, and it is
owner-executed — nothing in the codebase can do it.

Realistic goal: **DR 0 → ~20 in 3–6 months** via 30–60 genuine, topically
relevant links. That's enough for Google to crawl the full site and let the
long-tail (city pages, sign pages, localized pages) start ranking.

---

## 1. Quick wins (do these first — a weekend of work)

Every profile/listing below is a real, legitimate link you control:

- **Google Business Profile** (if there's any business address) and **Bing Places**.
- **App-store presence:** when the mobile app ships, the **Google Play Store
  listing** links your domain (a strong, trusted link). Until then, list the
  web app on **Product Hunt**, **AlternativeTo**, **SaaSHub**, and **There's An
  AI For That** (the AI palm-reading angle qualifies).
- **Social profiles with domain links:** you have Instagram — add the site link
  in bio. Create X/Twitter, YouTube (even 1 video), LinkedIn company page,
  Pinterest (astrology does exceptionally well there). Each is a no-follow
  link, but they build the brand entity Google associates with the domain.
- **Startup/India directories:** YourStory company profile, Startup India
  registry (if registered), Crunchbase, IndiaBizList-type regional directories.
  Skip anything that charges for "guaranteed DA links" — see §6.
- **Quora + Reddit presence:** answer real astrology questions
  (r/vedicastrology, Quora topics "Kundli", "Vedic Astrology") linking to a
  *specific relevant page* (a /learn article, a city panchang) only when it
  genuinely answers the question. 2–3 quality answers/week.

## 2. The /learn hub is your link magnet — use it

Nobody links to a kundli generator; people link to **reference content**. The
6 /learn guides (nakshatras, manglik dosha, kaal sarp, rashi vs sun sign,
muhurat, navamsa) are written to be linkable references. For each:

- Share to the relevant subreddit/Quora question threads when on-topic.
- Pitch as a "further reading" link to astrology bloggers who cover the topic
  with thinner content (find them: Google the topic, look at pages 2–3 —
  smaller sites respond to outreach; page-1 sites won't).

## 3. Data-PR: the unique asset no competitor gives away

You compute real astronomical data. Package it as citable assets journalists
and bloggers link to:

- **"Shubh Muhurat dates 2026/2027"** — a definitive, computed list of wedding /
  griha-pravesh muhurat dates. Every Indian lifestyle/wedding publication
  writes this story annually and needs a source. Pitch it to wedding-planning
  sites (WedMeGood, WeddingWire India blogs) and regional news lifestyle desks.
- **City sunrise/panchang tables** — "Rahu Kaal timings for the 51 largest
  Indian cities, updated daily" is a legit reference resource for local news
  sites' panchang columns.
- **Festival date explainers** — "Why Diwali 2026 falls on X: the astronomy" —
  seasonal, evergreen re-pitchable every year.

Publish each as a /learn article first, then outreach with the *data* as the
hook ("we computed this from Swiss Ephemeris; feel free to cite/embed, here's
the methodology").

## 4. Guest content & partnerships

- **Astrology/spirituality blogs:** offer a genuinely good guest post ("How
  Swiss Ephemeris computes your kundli — and why free calculators disagree")
  with one contextual link. Target blogs that already link out to tools.
- **Wedding-industry partnerships:** kundli matching is a wedding product.
  Wedding planners, matrimony blogs, pandit-booking services — offer their
  audiences a co-branded matching link/widget.
- **Local astrologers:** many have thin websites. Offer a free embeddable
  "Today's Panchang for {their city}" widget that credits + links you.
- **College/community festivals:** astrology stalls/events often publish
  sponsor/partner pages — cheap, legitimate local links.

## 5. Outreach template (short beats long)

> Subject: computed muhurat dates for 2027 — free to cite
>
> Hi {name} — I run MyAstro360, where we compute panchang/muhurat data with
> Swiss Ephemeris (the engine professional astrologers use). We've published
> the full list of {topic} — {url}. If it's useful for your {their article},
> you're welcome to cite it; happy to provide the raw table too.
> — Roshan

Rules: personalize the first line to *their* page; one link; no "SEO
collaboration" language; follow up once after a week, then stop.

## 6. Do NOT do (these get sites penalized)

- ❌ Buying links, "DA 50+ guest post $30" marketplaces, Fiverr link packages
- ❌ PBNs (private blog networks) and "web 2.0" spam
- ❌ Mass directory submissions to irrelevant/foreign directories
- ❌ Reciprocal link schemes and comment spam
- ❌ Exact-match anchor text campaigns ("free kundli online" as every anchor —
  anchors should mostly be the brand name or the page title)

One good link from a real astrology/wedding/news site beats fifty junk links —
and junk links on a DR-0 domain are how you get filtered before you start.

## 7. Cadence & measurement

- **Weekly:** 2–3 outreach emails, 2–3 Quora/Reddit answers, 1 social post.
- **Monthly:** check Ahrefs free DR (ahrefs.com/website-authority-checker) and
  GSC → Links report; expect referring domains to grow by a handful/month.
- **Success markers:** GSC "Discovered – currently not indexed" shrinking,
  average position improving on non-brand queries, first page-1 rankings on
  long-tail ("panchang {smaller city}", "{nakshatra} meaning").
